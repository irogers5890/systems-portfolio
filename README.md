## Selected Work

### Liquid Valve Endurance Tester & Leak-Test Fixture
*Parker Hannifin, Precision Fluidics · 2026*

Requirements definition and hydraulic architecture for a closed-loop DI-water endurance tester running 30–60 valves simultaneously at 120 psig, with fluid held within ±5°F of ambient. Sized pump, chiller, reservoir, manifold, and instrumentation, and released drawings, schematics, and P&IDs.

Designed a companion pressure-decay leak-test fixture resolving below 0.1 sccm in under 0.5 cc trapped volume, using a guarded three-port seal architecture with probe geometry sized against ±0.001 in worst-case tolerance and MMC/LMC analysis.

### Contamination Root-Cause Investigations
*Parker Hannifin, Precision Fluidics · 2026*

Two independent investigations into recurring return failures with distinct contamination mechanisms. Isolated an orifice-corrosion source across supplier, stock, cleaning, and floor-process variables through designed experiments, and traced a separate foreign-object mechanism by teardown and comparative inspection against conforming parts.

### Refreshable Braille Tactile Display
*Forge, Northeastern University Hardware Product Lab · 2026*

A six-dot Braille cell producing the full 64-character set from **two actuators instead of six**, by encoding all eight pin states of each half-cell as discrete positions on a stepped rack. Both motors sit outboard and drive racks inward through a bevel geartrain, clearing the 6.25 mm cell footprint so cells tile at reading pitch.

Full self-authored drawing package. Cycle testing on the direct-drive build cut jam rate from roughly 15% to under 2% across 2,000+ cycles after opening guide clearance, adding lead-in chamfers, and moving from PLA to PETG.

### Vision-Guided Pan/Tilt Tracking System
*Independent · 2025–26* → [**Project details**](GIMBAL.md)

Closed-loop target tracking platform — mechanical gimbal, electronics, and control software. Raspberry Pi 5, OpenCV/ArUco detection, PCA9685 servo control over I²C, PID loops on both axes, and automated re-acquisition when the marker leaves frame.

### Gamma Shielding Test System
*Melagen Labs · 2024* → [**Project details**](MELAGEN.md)

Co-designed the test fixture and experimental protocol for evaluating a melanin–HDPE composite against an aluminum reference using Cs-137 sources. Attenuation comparable to aluminum at roughly one-third the density, validated by ANOVA and regression in Python.
